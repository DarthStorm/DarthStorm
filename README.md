## Cool Projects!
[**Slime Game**](https://github.com/DarthStorm/Slime-Game) — The ubiquitous 2d platformer. Everywhere at once, never finished, always in progress. I have put this all over social media, and it may be what I am known for.

[**Square Game**](https://github.com/DarthStorm/square-game) — The not-so-ubiquitous 2d platformer with roguelike mechanics. Development is more active here. Made in Godot ❤️

[**PowerPoint Macros**](https://github.com/DarthStorm/powerpoint-macros) and [**cas**](https://github.com/DarthStorm/cas) — One of my Year 12 [CAS Experiences](https://ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/curriculum/dp-core/creativity-activity-and-service/). Fun to make, not so fun to reflect on. The former contains just the VBA macros that I've created and used, while the latter also contains the exact presentation I used for you to take inspiration from.

[**Find and Replace**](https://github.com/DarthStorm/find-and-replace) — A mod for Geometry Dash that implements Find and Replace in the editor. Finally, time to replace all your blue and yello gravirty portals with green ones. (*no but seriously why do some of you use them outside of anti-cheese*)

[**FocusZone**](https://github.com/DarthStorm/FocusZone) — Another Pomodoro timer clone which *some of you* need.

[**The Enemies Think**](https://github.com/DarthStorm/the-enemies-think) — A top-down run-and-gun game, built with pure JS — no engines, just `<canvas>` magic. Play [here](https://darthstorm.github.io/the-enemies-think/index.html)!
